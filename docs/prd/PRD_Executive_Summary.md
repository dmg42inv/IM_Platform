# IM Platform PRD - Executive Summary

## What This Product Is

The IM Platform is a unified operating system for institutional investment teams, covering deal pipeline, diligence, IC workflow, position management, monitoring, valuation, risk, compliance, and reporting.

## Why It Matters

The platform replaces fragmented spreadsheet and email-driven processes with auditable workflows, consistent data definitions, and faster decision cycles.

## Phased Delivery

- Phase 1: Institutional Core (identity, master data, pipeline, DD, IC, positions).
- Phase 2: Fund and LP Operations (monitoring, valuation, compliance, reporting).
- Phase 3: Multi-Asset Expansion and Intelligence (risk intelligence, advanced agent operations).

## Strategic Architecture Direction

- Target architecture is micro-services based, implemented progressively.
- Platform integrates API-first contracts, event-driven workflows, and strong observability.

## Intelligence and Agent Strategy

- Agents track internal file changes and investment state changes.
- Agents ingest macro/news signals and map potential impact to portfolios.
- High-impact recommendations remain human-approved.

## Data and Governance Strategy

- Relational, analytical, and document stores are used based on workload fit.
- Investment ontology and taxonomy maintain semantic consistency.
- PRD is versioned and governed through a formal change log and review cadence.

## Success Targets

- Faster deal-to-decision cycle times.
- Reduced reconciliation effort.
- High approval traceability and audit readiness.
- Reliable, repeatable portfolio reporting.
