What "verified" means here

Confidence levelWhat it means
Confirmed (signed document)Cross-checked directly against an executed SPA, Capital Account Statement, loan agreement, or equivalent primary source.
Confirmed (public filing)For listed portfolio companies, corroborated against the company's own audited/regulatory filing - independently reliable.
Shallow / AI-extractedSourced from an internal summary referencing a document that has not yet been directly read - flagged honestly, not presented as equally solid.
Tracker-sourced fallbackUsed only when no primary source exists yet for a structural fact - visibly flagged as such in the report, not silently blended in.
